## Design Direction: "Explorer's Journal"

The visual identity draws from adventure journals, treasure maps, and nature field guides. Hand-drawn textures meet clean digital UI. The feel is: **Indiana Jones meets Pokemon Go meets a beautifully illustrated children's book.**

Color palette per age band:
- **Primary (7-9):** Warm amber/gold, coral red, emerald green — treasure and nature
- **Intermediate (10-12):** Sky blue, teal, sunny amber — exploration and discovery
- **Teen (13-17):** Indigo, electric pink, dark teal — mystery and cool factor
- **Adult (18+):** Navy, sage green, warm gray — sophisticated field guide

## Notes for Generation

- All illustrations should have a **consistent style** — same artist feel across all pieces
- Avoid photorealism; lean into illustrated/artistic style
- Keep backgrounds clean enough that text can overlay them
- For hero images, ensure the top and bottom edges fade to white/transparent for seamless page integration
- Challenge type icons should be simple enough to be recognizable at 40x40px display size
- Generate at 2x the listed sizes for retina displays (e.g., 1200x400 → generate at 2400x800)
